DEOFPCSPIERCESubroutine

Compute the tentative principal components; they are 'tentative' because they might be the PCs of the switched data. Put them into 'tentpcs', which is of size (nt,nmodes) if we are doing regular EOFs and (nx,nmodes) if we are doing switched EOFs. These PCs come out in order corresponding to the order of 'evecs', which is in ASCENDING order.
